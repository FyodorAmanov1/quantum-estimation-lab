# Primary references and implementation boundaries

- [Mottonen, Vartiainen, Bergholm and Salomaa, Transformation of quantum states using uniformly controlled rotations (2005)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0407010): primary basis for the Gray-code rotation multiplexors in QE06. This application prepares only a real nonnegative state, retains zero-angle gates and counts its own emitted sequence; it does not use the paper's general complex-state gate totals.
- [Barenco et al., Elementary gates for quantum computation (1995)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9503016): primary background for controlled gates and reversible constructions. QE06 explicitly emits a clean AND ladder and retains CCX as a separate three-qubit primitive. Its mixed-level ledger is not a count of elementary two-qubit gates or a fault-tolerant resource estimate.

- [Clopper and Pearson, The use of confidence or fiducial limits illustrated in the case of the binomial (1934)](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/26.4.404): primary basis for the equal-tailed exact binomial baseline and Monte Carlo coverage uncertainty intervals in QE05. The likelihood intervals retain an asymptotic cutoff and have no corresponding finite-sample guarantee.

- [Original portfolio PFE project](https://github.com/FyodorAmanov1/QStockPrediction)
- [Amplitude amplification and estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0005055)
- [Suzuki et al., Amplitude estimation without phase estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.10246): primary basis for combining counts at different Grover powers by maximum likelihood. The implementation uses a finite theta mesh and a conditional asymptotic likelihood interval; no interval coverage or performance guarantee is inferred from this citation.
- [Woerner and Egger, Quantum Risk Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.06893): primary background for encoding bounded financial functions into an ancilla probability. QE04 uses an empirical histogram of actual option-book exposure, exact node rotations via a dense Householder matrix, and separate cap/grid errors; it does not implement their full risk algorithms or a coherent Black-Scholes pricing circuit.
- [NumPy sample covariance definition](https://numpy.org/doc/1.20/reference/generated/numpy.cov.html): the adapter uses observations in rows and the N−1 normalization. Annualization by 252 is an explicit equal-trading-step assumption.
- [Ledoit and Wolf, Honey, I Shrunk the Sample Covariance Matrix (2004)](https://ledoit.net/Honey_2004.pdf): primary background on covariance shrinkage. This application uses a fixed 10% diagonal target; it does **not** implement their estimated optimal intensity or claim their empirical results.
- [Durham, SV mixture models with application to S&P 500 index returns (2007)](https://digitalcommons.calpoly.edu/fin_fac/15/), Journal of Financial Economics 85, 822–856, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2006.06.005): primary background on discrete normal mixtures and conditional return shape. QE03 uses a specified two-state horizon variance mixture, not Durham's fitted dynamic model or its empirical findings. The variance normalization, innovation kurtosis and conditional expected-spot identities are derived explicitly in [QE03](reports/QE03.md).

The implementation, data provenance and scope limits are documented in [the technical case study](reports/applied-study.md). Citation identifies an algorithmic foundation, not equivalence with the full paper or a claim of hardware performance.
